Ultimate Design · proposal · 2026-10-08

A design tool, not a chat with a canvas

The studio holds many design systems, palettes, layouts and mockups. Its own look should stay out of their way: no hue of its own around the work, space only for what earns it, and the agent as one tool among several rather than the frame everything sits in. One new structure, three looks to pick from.

What is wrong now

It reads as Claude

Cream paper, a serif display face, a soft indigo button and a chat column on the left: the anatomy of Claude.ai.

The chrome tints the work

A warm cream surround shifts how every design's colours read. For a tool that judges palettes and design systems, that is a defect, not a style.

Space is not earned

The create forms take a quarter of Home permanently; the studio stacks model, palette, cost, techniques and system controls above the chat; the version slider spans the whole width.

The pieces are scattered

Design systems sit in the project grid, Library and Rules are separate pages, findings arrive as chat cards, and approval sits on the version bar.

One structure, whichever look

A section rail

  • Projects, Systems, Library, Rules (with its inbox count).
  • Design systems get their own section: tokens, components, specimen, the designs that use them.
  • A wide search, always visible, across all four.

Home is the work

  • No create column: one “New…” opens a sheet (design, design system, from a prompt).
  • Large thumbnails with their state: approved, findings, system, palette.
  • Filters by state and by system.

Studio is canvas-first

  • Left: pages, directions and options of the project.
  • Centre: the design at true colour on a neutral surround.
  • Right: one panel, tabs Agent · Inspect · Findings · History. Versions become thumbnails in History.
  • Top: view, width, and the one primary action, Approve for build. Model, cost and references move into a project popover.

Three looks

A · Workbench

Geist · zero-chroma greys · ink primary

The quiet professional tool. Greys with no hue at all, so nothing around a design shifts its colours; black ink for the one primary action; one blue for selection. Dense, small type, everything a click away.

Trade-off: the most neutral and the least personal. It could be any good design tool.

studioprojects

B · Nightshift

Rexorious · Archivo + Chivo Mono · Volt once per view

Your own identity as the tool's chassis: void and chassis greys, stencilled mono labels, zero radius, a notched primary. Kept restrained around the work: Volt is spent once per view (Approve for build) and on the selection edge.

Trade-off: unmistakably yours, and loud chrome is a risk next to a quiet design; a dark surround also makes light designs look brighter than on a white page.

studioprojects

C · Lightbox

IBM Plex · proofing grey · floating pills

The board is the whole screen, on the mid grey printers judge colour against. Every control floats in a small white pill; the agent is a command bar at the bottom that opens into its thread. Home is a contact sheet.

Trade-off: the most room for the work and the most different from today; floating panels cover the board, and the agent thread is a step further away.

What is not decided here

The decisions are in docs/gate-studio-redesign.md: the look, and each structural change on its own, so you can take the structure without a look or mix (say, A's chrome with C's board). Nothing in the app changes until you pick.
